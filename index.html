Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 26 July 2026

Thank you for using Clerking App.

Clerking App is designed to help medical students and healthcare professionals perform structured patient clerking by providing symptom-based history-taking prompts and reference information.

Your privacy is important to us.

Information We Collect

Clerking App does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information.

The app does not require you to create an account or provide personal details.

Medical Information

Clerking App does not ask you to enter or upload patient-identifiable information.

Users are responsible for ensuring that no confidential patient information is entered into the app if future versions introduce note-taking or other data-entry features.

Internet Connection

Most features of Clerking App work offline.

If future versions introduce online features, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

Advertising

If the app displays advertisements, they may be provided by third-party advertising services such as Google AdMob.

These services may collect anonymous information, such as:

  • Device information
  • Advertising identifiers
  • App usage information
  • Crash reports

This information is collected by the advertising provider and is governed by their own privacy policies.

For more information, please refer to Google's Privacy Policy.

Analytics

Clerking App does not currently use analytics services to track user behaviour.

If analytics are added in future updates, this Privacy Policy will be revised.

Data Security

Because Clerking App does not collect personal information, we do not store personal data on our servers.

Children's Privacy

Clerking App is not specifically directed at children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
